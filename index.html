Who we are

Cinema can help us travel, not only to different places but towards one another.

Passenger Cinema was founded in London in 2024 on a simple idea: that a film can put you somewhere you have never been, next to someone you would never otherwise have met.

Unlike most community cinemas, we don’t have a permanent venue. Every event starts with the same question: where should this film live? Finding a new home for every screening is our biggest challenge, and it has become our greatest strength. Our audience follows us wherever we go: an independent cinema, a film school, a 1920s picture palace, a basement in Dalston, a craft café. And every new venue introduces Passenger Cinema to a different local community.

No two Passenger Cinema events are the same. That keeps us creatively challenged, and it keeps our audience curious about where we’ll take them next.

01 / Travel

Travelling the world through films

Films transport us to different places and immerse us in new cultures without leaving our seats. With each screening we bring that sense of exploration and cultural discovery, one country at a time.

02 / Community

A hub, not an audience

People from very different backgrounds connect over the universal language of cinema. Our screenings are a space to meet, exchange stories and build connections that outlast the credits.

03 / Culture

Celebration, not tourism

We welcome people who are part of the culture on screen alongside those discovering it for the first time. That mix is the whole point: it’s how a screening becomes empathy rather than spectacle.
