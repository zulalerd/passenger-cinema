Where should we go next?

Our best events have started with someone telling us about a film we’d never heard of, or offering a room nobody had thought of using as a cinema. Both are below.

01 / Recommend a film

It doesn’t need to be obscure and it doesn’t need to be worthy. It needs to be worth building an event around. Tell us why it matters to you.

If it’s a film from a culture you belong to, say so, because we would love to make it with you rather than about you.

02 / Offer a space

We have screened in a basement music venue, a members’ bar, a 1920s picture palace, a 16mm film club, a film school, a bookshop cinema and a craft café on its opening weekend.

You don’t need a projector, a screen or blackout blinds. We have brought all three before, along with a bedsheet and a lot of volunteers.
