About

We go wherever the film belongs.

Passenger Cinema was founded in London in 2024 on a simple idea: cinema can help us travel, not only to different places but towards one another.

The idea

Every screening is an invitation to discover a new country, meet new people and experience a culture through film.

Our programme reflects the diversity of the city we call home. Every event brings together people of different ages, nationalities and backgrounds around a shared experience, proving that cinema can create common ground even when we don’t share the same language or story.

We have travelled through cinema to Turkey, Japan, Brazil, Mexico, Jamaica, Italy, Hong Kong, Spain and Iran, celebrating stories that are rarely given the spotlight in mainstream cinemas. We believe audiences are curious and adventurous, and our programming reflects that. We have been proud to welcome people from the cultures represented on screen alongside those discovering them for the first time, creating conversations that continue long after the credits roll.

Solidarity

Jamaica

During our 16mm screening of The Harder They Come at Ciné-Real in Hackney, we donated half of all ticket proceeds to support Jamaican hurricane relief, giving audiences a way to respond with solidarity as well as curiosity.

Barcelona

We brought British cinema to audiences in Barcelona through a screening and a culinary experience inspired by The Cook, the Thief, His Wife & Her Lover, organised with local community builders and food creatives.

Two cities

London & Istanbul

We connected the two great cities we call home through simultaneous screenings of The Red Shoes, followed by a live-streamed discussion with Central Film School that let audiences in both cities share the same story and the same conversation at the same time.
