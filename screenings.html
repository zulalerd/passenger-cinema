What’s on

We programme one journey at a time, and we build the whole event around it: the room, the food, the music, the people who know the film best.

If it’s your first time

What actually happens at a Passenger Cinema event?

Before

Come on your own

Plenty of people do. Doors open early on purpose so there is time to get a drink and talk to someone before the lights go down.

During

A short introduction, then the film

We say a few words about why we chose it and who helped us bring it here. Then we get out of the way.

After

The part people remember

A workshop, a meal, a live set, a Q&A, a conversation that runs long. This is the half of the event that turns an audience into a room of people who know each other.
