Passenger Cinema is a volunteer-led community, and we want everyone who contributes to feel that they are genuinely part of what we are building.

What we offer

We hope Passenger Cinema can be a space where you can experiment, meet like-minded people and become part of a growing community built around cinema, culture and storytelling.

  • 01Shape Passenger Cinema with us. Contribute to our projects, screenings and events, and have a real voice in how the community develops.
  • 02Hands-on experience working inside an independent cultural organisation, involved in the creative process behind our events.
  • 03Meet and collaborate with filmmakers, artists and creatives working across cinema, culture and the arts.
  • 04Attend our screenings and events as part of the team.
  • 05Build your portfolio. Use the work you make with Passenger Cinema in your own professional or creative practice.
  • 06Space for your own creative work. If you’re an artist or filmmaker, we want Passenger Cinema to be a platform for your creativity too: your illustrations or artwork in our posters and visual materials, your short films screened at our events, or another way of bringing your work into the room.
  • 07An annual membership to The Prince Charles Cinema, so you have another space to keep watching and discovering all year.
