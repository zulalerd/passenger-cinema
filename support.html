Support us

Help us keep travelling.

Passenger Cinema is run entirely by volunteers. Nobody takes a wage. What we raise goes straight back into the next screening.

Where it goes

Every event has to pay for itself before it can happen. A licence for the film, a room for the day, a projector and a sound system if the room hasn’t got them, and the posters that get people through the door.

Ticket prices cover most of it, and we keep them deliberately low so that money is never the reason someone doesn’t come. Donations are what close the gap, and what let us take a risk on a film nobody has heard of.
